A quiet window every day — defaults to 8 PM – 6 AM

In the window the buddy is silent (no emotion sounds, no cuckoo — alarms and reminders still ring) and, if it fell asleep during the window, it wakes up on its own when the window ends. Sleeping works any time of day now: left alone on its home face for 15 min it drifts to its sleeping face, at 30 min the screen goes dark. Alarms and reminders wake it — after the ring it goes back to sleep. Widgets, menus and study sessions are never auto-slept.
